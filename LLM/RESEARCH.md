# RESEARCH.md

### Equity Research Group

### Mission
The Equity Research Group is a student-run equity research organization focused on under-covered small and mid-cap companies. The group produces institutional-quality research on names that receive little Wall Street coverage, developing members into rigorous fundamental analysts.

---

## Investment Universe

- Market cap: $300M – $5B
- All sectors, all geographies (US-listed)
- No style mandate — value, growth, and GARP approaches are all welcome depending on the opportunity

---

## Core Edge

### Deep primary research on ignored names
Sell-side coverage drops off dramatically below $5B market cap. The group's edge is simple: we do the work others don't.

This means going beyond filings and earnings calls to include:

- customer interviews
- competitor analysis
- supply chain mapping
- on-the-ground research where possible

---

## Structure

### Year One: Flat Partnership
The group launches with 15–20 sector leads operating as equals. No junior analysts until the model is proven.

### Executive Board
Three functional roles with administrative responsibilities. All executive board members also maintain sector coverage and pitch like everyone else.

#### President
- Sets meeting cadence and deadlines
- Runs weekly meetings
- Represents the group externally
- Maintains sector coverage and pitches

#### Portfolio Coordinator
- Tracks paper portfolio positions and performance
- Logs entry prices, exit prices, and catalysts
- Runs quarterly performance attribution
- Maintains sector coverage and pitches

#### Publishing Lead
- Manages Substack and external communications
- Edits memos for public release
- Coordinates quarterly research report
- Maintains sector coverage and pitches

## Membership

- 15–20 members in year one
- Admission by application, stock pitch submission, and interview
- Every member pitches at least once per semester — no exceptions
- Inactive members are removed at semester end

---

## Research Process

### Idea Generation
- Screens
- 13F tracking
- Industry reading
- Personal observation

Each lead maintains a watchlist of 10+ names in their sector.

Weekly sector updates surface early-stage ideas for group feedback.

### Research Standard
Every pitch must include:

- business overview and industry context
- variant perception — what is the market missing?
- key drivers and risks
- financial model with base, bull, and bear cases
- valuation and price target
- defined catalyst or timeline

### Pitch Format
- Written memo (10–15 pages)
- 30-minute presentation + 15-minute Q&A
- All leads vote: Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, or Short
- Majority vote required for paper portfolio inclusion

---

## Portfolio Management

- Paper portfolio with $1M notional starting capital
- Position sizes: 3–7% per name at entry
- Maximum 20 positions at any time
- Positions reviewed quarterly — leads must defend or exit
- All trades logged with rationale

---

## Output

### Internal
- All memos archived in shared drive
- Portfolio tracked in real time with entry dates and prices
- Quarterly review of past calls: wins, losses, lessons learned

### External
- Substack publication with polished research
- Quarterly report summarizing top ideas and portfolio performance

### Goal
Build a public track record members can reference in recruiting.

---

## Meeting Cadence

- Weekly meetings (1.5 hours)
- One or two pitches per week, rotating through sectors
- Remaining time: sector updates, market discussion, and idea generation
- Attendance is mandatory; two unexcused absences trigger removal review

---

## Member Expectations

Every member commits to:

- a minimum of one full pitch per semester
- ongoing coverage and watchlist maintenance for their sector
- active participation in Q&A and voting
- tracking personal calls and learning from mistakes

---

## Recruiting Positioning

This group exists to prepare members for equity research careers. By graduation, every member should have:

- 4+ polished, interview-ready stock pitches
- deep expertise in at least one sector
- a public track record via Substack
- clear perspectives on markets and individual names

---

## Future: Year Two and Beyond

Once the model is proven, the group will add a junior analyst layer:

- sector leads become mentors, each managing 1–2 analysts
- analysts complete a training program and pitch to earn voting membership
- structure scales to 30–40 members while maintaining quality
- analyst-to-lead promotion is based on pitch quality and commitment

---

## Differentiation

Unlike broad investment clubs that rotate members through generic projects, NSCR gives each member true ownership of a coverage universe.

The small-cap focus ensures original research rather than regurgitation of Street consensus.

The flat structure in year one creates accountability — there is nowhere to hide.
